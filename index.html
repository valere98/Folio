Behind the build

Good engineering is what makes a polished experience dependable.

A considered interface is one part of the work. The information, integrations, and decisions behind it matter just as much.

01 · Foundations

Data & APIs

Start with the information and actions the product actually needs.

02 · Trust

Identity & access

Plan authentication and permissions around the people using the system.

03 · Connections

Integrations

Connect external services deliberately, with clear expectations and failure states.

04 · Delivery

Deployment

Ship in a way that is maintainable, observable, and appropriate to the project.

05 · Safety

Security & privacy

Consider access, data handling, and user trust throughout the build.

06 · Experience

Usability

Make common tasks understandable, accessible, and comfortable across screens.
