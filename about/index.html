The person behind the work

Design minded engineering for human sized problems.

I’m Valere Mbaya, a software developer and digital designer based in Nairobi. I bring visual craft and practical engineering together to make digital tools easier to understand and use.

How I work

Start with the people. Stay curious about the details.

I like work where the goal is bigger than “make it look modern.” What does someone need to do? What information helps them decide? Which parts should feel simple, and which need stronger foundations behind the scenes?

Those questions help me move between interface design, content, data, integrations, and implementation without losing sight of the people at the other end.

Principles

Good work should feel considered.

A few values I try to bring to every collaboration.

01

Clarity before complexity

Understand the need first. Add technology only where it earns its place.

02

Honest by default

Be clear about what is live, what is a concept, and what still needs validation.

03

Learn in the open

Stay curious, test assumptions, and share useful lessons from the build.

04

Respect the details

Accessibility, responsive behavior, content, and edge cases are part of the experience.

Let’s make something useful.
